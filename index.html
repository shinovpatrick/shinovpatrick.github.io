04 · ABOUT

Hi, I'm Shinov.

I am a Geospatial Scientist and PhD researcher working at the intersection of Geospatial Intelligence, Earth Observation, Artificial Intelligence and Disaster Science.

My research focuses on developing innovative geospatial and machine learning frameworks for flood risk assessment, hazard modelling, disaster resilience and sustainable environmental management, integrating multi-source spatial data with advanced analytical techniques.

My broader interests include geospatial technology, disaster management, spatial planning, geography, GIS, remote sensing, GeoAI and environmental applications of spatial data.
